# Content Editing

Read before writing. Use registered_meta to understand REST-visible custom fields and search_content/edit_content for token-efficient exact replacements. Keep match-once safety unless replacement of every match is intentional; preserve post status, slug, taxonomy, featured media, and builder metadata.

## Workflow

Inspect current state first, make the smallest supported change, verify the result, and preserve WordPress/WPVibe capability and approval checks.
